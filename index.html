TOEvang. Oladeru
Evang. Oladeru,

Appointment as Diaspora Chairman

Warm Christian greetings to you in the precious name of our Lord and Saviour, Jesus Christ.

It is with great pleasure that the Church Committee of CCC Ijesha Parish IV formally communicates to you the decision of the Committee to appoint you as one of the Diaspora Chairmen of the Parish.

This appointment carries the responsibility of providing leadership, coordination, and support to members of our Parish residing outside Nigeria, particularly those in Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, and other parts of the world.

After careful consideration, the Committee is confident that your experience, dedication, commitment to the Church, and cordial relationship with our members make you well suited for this important responsibility. We believe that your contribution will help foster stronger communication, greater participation, unity, fellowship, and a deeper sense of belonging among our members in the Diaspora.

As you assume this responsibility, we encourage you to work closely with the Parish leadership and fellow Diaspora Chairman in carrying out this assignment. We look forward to your valuable input in building a stronger connection between our Diaspora members and the Parish, while supporting the spiritual growth and overall progress of the Church.

We trust that you will approach this responsibility with humility, wisdom, dedication, and the fear of God. We also encourage you to see this appointment not merely as a position, but as an opportunity to serve God, strengthen His Church, and positively impact the lives of our members wherever they may be.

On behalf of the Church Committee, we warmly congratulate you on this appointment and pray that the Almighty God will grant you abundant grace, wisdom, strength, understanding, and divine direction. May the Lord prosper your efforts, enlarge your capacity for service, and use you as an instrument of unity, fellowship, and spiritual growth among our members in the Diaspora.

We look forward to your cooperation and meaningful contribution in this new capacity.

Congratulations once again, and may the Lord continue to bless, uphold, and strengthen you in His service.

Yours faithfully in Christ,

For: The Church Committee
